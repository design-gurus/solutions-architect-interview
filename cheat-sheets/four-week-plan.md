# Four-Week Preparation Plan

This plan prepares every round of a solutions architect (SA) loop in four weeks at 8 to 10 hours a week. It assumes an engineer who already knows one cloud platform. Each week ends with a finish condition, which is a test that shows the week's work is complete.

Confirm the loop with the recruiter first. If the loop has no presentation or no coding assignment, move those hours to the design round.

## The four weeks at a glance

| Week | Focus | Hours | Finish condition |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | The loop, the design method, and breadth | 9 | One design case finished in 45 minutes with a cost estimate |
| 2 | Design cases and cost | 10 | Five cases finished, each with a stated trade-off |
| 3 | Discovery, objections, and stories | 9 | Eight stories written, and three role-plays completed with a partner |
| 4 | The presentation and full practice | 10 | One timed presentation and one full practice loop |

## Week 1: the loop, the method, and breadth

| Task | Hours |
|---|---:|
| Read [the interview loop](../rounds/interview-loop.md) and the page for your target company | 1 |
| Learn the ten steps in the [design round framework](design-round-framework.md) | 2 |
| Write three-layer answers for 30 screen questions, five in each of the six areas | 4 |
| Complete one design case with a timer | 2 |

**Finish condition:** one design case completed in 45 minutes, with 11 minutes spent on questions and a monthly cost estimate at the end.

## Week 2: design cases and cost

| Task | Hours |
|---|---:|
| Work through the [cost estimation](cost-estimation.md) and [migration and disaster recovery](migration-and-disaster-recovery.md) sheets | 2 |
| Complete five design cases from the [question bank](question-bank.md), one a day | 6 |
| Say 10 screen answers aloud each day, 90 seconds each | 2 |

Choose the five cases to cover different shapes: one migration, one disaster recovery, one multi-tenant platform, one cost reduction, and one data platform.

**Finish condition:** five cases, each closed with one sentence that names the trade-off and the rejected option.

## Week 3: discovery, objections, and stories

| Task | Hours |
|---|---:|
| Read the [customer discovery role-play](../rounds/customer-discovery-role-play.md) and [behavioral](../rounds/behavioral.md) pages | 1 |
| Write eight stories of two minutes each, including one lost deal and one mistake | 4 |
| Run three role-plays with a partner who plays a different customer each time | 3 |
| Record two stories and listen for "we" where "I" belongs | 1 |

In each role-play, ask at least five questions before proposing anything. Ask the partner to raise one objection on cost and one on security.

**Finish condition:** every story family has two stories, and each role-play ended with a summary that the partner corrected.

## Week 4: the presentation and full practice

| Task | Hours |
|---|---:|
| Build an eight-slide talk from the plan on the [presentation page](../rounds/presentation.md) | 3 |
| Rehearse aloud three times with a timer | 2 |
| Present to a partner who interrupts twice and asks four follow-up questions | 1 |
| Run one full practice loop: screen, design, role-play, and behavioral | 3 |
| Review the [common mistakes](common-mistakes.md) the day before | 1 |

**Finish condition:** the talk runs 18 minutes for a 20-minute slot, and the recommendation is on the first slide.

## If you have less time

| Time available | Keep | Drop |
|---|---|---|
| Two weeks | Three design cases, six stories, and the presentation | The 30 written screen answers, replaced by reading the table of six areas |
| One week | One design case a day and the presentation | Role-plays with a partner, replaced by answering three objections aloud alone |
| Three days | The presentation, three stories, and the design framework | Everything else |

The presentation stays in every version, because it is the round that engineers moving into the role have practiced least.

## Company adjustments

| Company | Change to the plan |
|---|---|
| AWS | Move 3 hours from design to stories, and map each story to the Leadership Principles |
| Databricks or Snowflake | Add 4 hours of SQL and data frame practice in weeks 1 and 2 |
| Microsoft | Prepare one adoption story with numbers in week 3 |
| Google Cloud | Prepare one technical win story, and review before each stage |
