# Solutions Architect Interview Question Bank

Fifty practice questions for the solutions architect (SA) interview, grouped by round. Each group starts with one line on what a strong answer contains. Company loops combine these rounds in different ways, so confirm the format with the recruiter.

## Architecture design (10)

A strong answer asks questions first, then covers numbers, design, failure, security, cost, and the order of the move.

1. "A travel booking company runs its website on 14 servers in its own data center, and the contract ends in 9 months. How would you move it to the cloud?"
2. "A payroll service runs in one cloud region. Design disaster recovery that survives the loss of that region."
3. "Design a platform that serves 3,000 business customers and keeps each customer's data apart."
4. "Our cloud bill has doubled in two years. The board wants it 40 percent lower within one quarter. Where do we start?"
5. "A bank must connect its data center to the cloud. Design the network."
6. "Design a data lake and analytics platform for a company with 12 source systems."
7. "Design an order processing system with no servers to manage."
8. "Ingest readings from one million devices and alert on faults within one minute."
9. "A healthcare application must keep patient data inside one country. Design the hosting."
10. "A large enterprise has 40 teams starting in the cloud at once. Design the account structure and the controls they all share."

## Technical screen (12)

A strong answer has three layers in about 90 seconds: a short answer, the mechanism, and the trade-off.

11. "What is the difference between a public subnet and a private subnet?"
12. "How does a private server download updates?"
13. "What is the difference between a security group and a network access control list?"
14. "What is the shared responsibility model?"
15. "What is the difference between a read replica and a standby?"
16. "When would you choose a key-value database over a relational one?"
17. "When would you put a queue between two services?"
18. "When would you choose containers over virtual machines, and functions over containers?"
19. "What is the difference between object storage, block storage, and file storage?"
20. "How does a content delivery network reduce load on the origin?"
21. "What is the difference between a data warehouse and a data lake?"
22. "How does a model answer questions from a company's own documents?"

## Customer discovery and objections (10)

A strong answer asks a question before it answers one, then gives evidence the customer can check.

23. "Our data is safer in our own data center. Why would we hand it to a cloud provider?"
24. "Your platform costs 30 percent more than a competitor's. What do you say?"
25. "We are worried about being locked in to one vendor."
26. "My CEO has already announced the date. Can you make it work?"
27. "We already use your competitor. Why should we change?"
28. "Just show me a demonstration. I do not have time for questions."
29. "Our engineers have never run anything in the cloud. How is this going to work?"
30. "Your last customer in our industry had an outage. Why should we trust the platform?"
31. "We do not know how many requests we handle. Can you size it anyway?"
32. "The security team will never approve this. What now?"

## Presentation follow-ups (8)

A strong answer gives the direct answer in the first sentence, then one piece of support.

33. "Is the 20 percent saving yearly or monthly?"
34. "Why not keep fixed servers and size them for the peak?"
35. "While we move, should we also replace our finance system?"
36. "Cloud bills always grow. Your saving is a sales number."
37. "What will the vendor charge for licenses on cloud servers?"
38. "What is the largest risk in this plan, and who owns it?"
39. "What happens if the migration is late?"
40. "Which option did you reject, and why?"

## Behavioral (10)

A strong answer is a real story of about two minutes with numbers, your own part named, and a change in how you work.

41. "Tell me about a time a customer was unhappy with your work or your product."
42. "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a customer."
43. "Tell me about a time you were overruled."
44. "Tell me about a deal you lost."
45. "Tell me about a deal you helped win."
46. "Tell me about a time you told a customer no."
47. "Tell me about a time you had to learn a technology quickly for a customer."
48. "Tell me about a time you explained a technical decision to an executive."
49. "Tell me about a mistake you made in front of a customer."
50. "Why this role, and why this company?"

## How to use this bank

| Days | Practice |
|---|---|
| Each day | One design question in 45 minutes, with a cost estimate |
| Each day | Five screen questions aloud, 90 seconds each |
| Twice a week | Three objections with a partner who plays the customer |
| Once a week | A full presentation with four follow-up questions |
