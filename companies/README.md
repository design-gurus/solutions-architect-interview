# Companies

Five reported solutions architect (SA) loops, side by side. Only Databricks publishes its process. Every other row comes from candidate reports, preparation guides, or the company's general careers pages, so treat each detail as commonly reported and confirm it with the recruiter. This guide is not affiliated with any company listed.

| Company | Role title | Reported loop | Distinctive round | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AWS | Solutions architect | Recruiter call, optional online assessment, phone screen, final loop of about five rounds, presentation | Behavioral rounds based on the Leadership Principles | [aws.md](aws.md) |
| Microsoft | Cloud solution architect | 2 to 4 conversations of up to an hour each | Stories about adoption after the sale | [microsoft.md](microsoft.md) |
| Google Cloud | Customer engineer | 4 to 5 interviews, including a design and a presentation | A design that supports a sale | [google-cloud.md](google-cloud.md) |
| Databricks | Solutions architect | Eight published steps, about 5.5 to 6.5 hours of interviews | A coding assignment and a 1-hour presentation with discovery | [databricks.md](databricks.md) |
| Snowflake | Sales engineer or solutions engineer | Screens, a take-home task, and a final presentation | A live demonstration | [snowflake.md](snowflake.md) |

## Three patterns across the five

1. **Cloud providers test breadth.** Their screens cover compute, storage, networking, security, and databases across the whole platform.
2. **Data platforms test practical work.** Both data platform loops are reported to include a task that the candidate completes alone.
3. **Almost every loop ends with a presentation.** Its length ranges from 20 minutes to 1 hour in the reports.

## AI companies

Some AI companies post a similar role as applied AI architect. They publish no loop, and public reports are few, so this guide has no page for them. Their postings stress discovery, prototypes, evaluations, and example code that customers can extend. If that work appeals to you, also read the free [Forward Deployed Engineer Interview](https://github.com/design-gurus/forward-deployed-engineer-interview) guide.
